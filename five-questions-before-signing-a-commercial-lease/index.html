Five Questions to Ask Before Signing a Commercial Lease in Dubai

Posted on September 8, 2026 · By Lantern Gate Editorial Team

A commercial lease is usually one of the biggest fixed costs a small business signs up to. Before you put pen to paper, take an hour to ask these five questions. They will not replace advice on your own contract, but they will help you notice problems early.

1. Who is the landlord, and do they have the right to lease?

Ask for the title or ownership documents and check that the name on the lease matches. If the unit is sub-let, ask for the landlord’s written permission.

2. What exactly is included in the rent?

List what you pay on top of the annual rent: service charges, parking, chilled water, maintenance and any security deposit. Ask when each is due and what happens if the amounts change.

3. How can the lease end, and what does it cost to leave early?

Read the termination clause twice. Note the notice period, any penalty for leaving before the end of the term, and who is responsible for restoring the unit to its original condition.

4. What happens at renewal?

Many tenants are surprised by a rent increase at renewal. Ask how the new rent is set and how much notice the landlord must give you before changing the terms.

5. Is the tenancy contract registered?

In Dubai, tenancy contracts are registered through the Ejari system. A registered contract helps you prove your tenancy when you apply for a trade licence or need to resolve a dispute.

Keep a copy of the signed lease, the payment receipts and every email about the unit in one folder. If something goes wrong later, those papers will matter.

Finally, give yourself time. A landlord who is in a hurry for your signature is a good reason to slow down. A reasonable landlord will answer your questions and let you take the draft home to read it calmly.

This article is general information, not legal advice. Please speak to a lawyer about your own lease.
