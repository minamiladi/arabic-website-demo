Lantern Gate Advocates & Legal Consultants was founded in Dubai in 2011. We advise start-ups, family businesses, investors and private clients, and we work in English and Arabic from the first call to the final signature.

Our lawyers explain each step in plain language, give you a clear fee estimate before work starts, and stay reachable throughout. Whether you are opening a company, buying an apartment or facing a dispute, we will help you decide what to do next.

Why clients choose Lantern Gate

  • Fees agreed in advance. You receive a written estimate and we tell you if the scope changes.
  • Senior lawyers on every file. A partner reviews all advice before it reaches you.
  • Bilingual documents. We prepare contracts and letters in English and Arabic, so everyone signs the same meaning.

Most new clients start with a short call. We listen, ask a few questions and tell you honestly whether we are the right firm for your matter.
